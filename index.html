Features

Everything your PDF needs

A complete PDF toolkit inside a sleek Chrome extension. No external server handles your documents.

✏️
Text Editing
Detect and modify existing text blocks. Preserve original fonts, sizes and positions. Apply your own styling.
✍️
E-Signatures
Draw your signature, type it in cursive, or upload an image. Place and resize anywhere on the page.
🔀
Merge PDFs
Combine multiple PDF files into one. Drag to reorder, include your current edits in the final document.
✂️
Extract Pages
Select specific pages and export them as a standalone PDF. Thumbnail preview for easy selection.
🖍️
Annotations
Draw highlight rectangles in any colour and opacity. Non-destructive annotations baked into the final PDF.
⚡
Convert
Export pages as JPG, PNG, DOCX, TXT, HTML or CSV. Batch export all pages in one click.
